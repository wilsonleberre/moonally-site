Getting Started

Your First Two Weeks

Give your body time to adapt — here's what to expect.

Days 1–3

Start short

Start with 20–30 minute sessions. Your body is adjusting to a new position, and mild shin pressure in the first few days is expected and temporary.

Week 1–2

Extend and tighten

Extend your sitting time gradually. After the first week, tighten the cross-bolts as the oak settles into place for a more secure fit.

Week 2+

Rotate

Settle into longer, well-supported sessions. Rotate between kneeling, sitting cross-legged, and standing to keep the day moving.

How to Use

Active Sitting, Done Right

A few habits make all the difference.

01

Posture

Keep your hips slightly higher than your knees and let the rocking base do small, natural work — don't force stillness. A neutral, moving spine beats a rigid, upright one.

02

Height Adjustment

Set seat height so your forearms rest level with your desk. Reset it any time you switch between sitting and standing at your desk — it takes seconds, no tools needed.

03

Daily Rotation

Alternate kneeling, cross-legged sitting, and standing through the day. MoonAlly is built for variety, not for replacing every other way you sit.
