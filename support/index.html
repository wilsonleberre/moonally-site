Your First Two Weeks

Give your body time to adapt — here's what to expect.

Days 1–3

Start with 20–30 min sessions. Your body is adapting — some shin pressure is completely normal.

Week 1–2

Gradually extend your sitting time. Tighten the cross-bolts after the first week as the wood settles.

Week 2+

Enjoy longer, more supported sessions. Rotate between kneeling, sitting cross-legged, and standing.

Active Sitting, Done Right

A few habits make all the difference.

Posture

Hips open at ~110°, spine naturally upright. Let the rocking base do the work — micro-movements engage your core passively.

Height Adjustment

Tool-free adjustment. Set the seat so your hips sit slightly higher than your knees. Works best with desks 29–35 inches tall.

Daily Rotation

2–4 hrs kneeling → 30 min standing → 2–4 hrs kneeling. Movement is the goal, not static sitting.
